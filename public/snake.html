SNAKEWAR
How to play

Arrows / WASD steer · Space use your next held item (host: start round) · Enter chat

Playing alone is classic snake: small board, solid walls, +1 per food, and your score (food eaten) is your high score.

With 2+ players: each food grows you by 2 (tiny yellow food by 1), food gets more plentiful as the round goes on, and the edges wrap around. Hitting any snake or blockade kills you and your body breaks into food. If two heads meet face to face both die; if you turn into someone's head from the side, only you die. After 2 minutes, or soon after someone dies, the board starts shrinking (10 s warning, new edge in red); anything left outside is cut off. Last snake (or team) alive wins.

Team mode (host's choice): random Red vs Blue. Reds are warm colors, blues are cool colors. Collect revive tokens: every 3 brings a fallen teammate back.

Between rounds, or while you're out, use the ‹ › arrows by your name to pick your color (in team mode, a shade of your team's color).
