SNAKEWAR
How to play

Playing alone is classic snake: small board, solid walls, +1 per food, and your score (food eaten) is your high score.

With 2+ players: each food grows you by 2 (tiny yellow food by 1), food gets more plentiful as the round goes on, and the edges wrap around. Hitting any snake or blockade kills you and your body breaks into food. If two heads meet face to face both die; if you turn into someone's head from the side, only you die. After 2 minutes, or soon after someone dies (team mode: or once a team is down to its last snake), the board starts shrinking (10 s warning, new edge in red); anything left outside is cut off. Last snake (or team) alive wins.

Team mode (host's choice): Red vs Blue, either random teams or players choose (with the color arrows by your name). Set your preferred team shade in ⚙ Settings; Red starts on the left, Blue on the right. Reds are warm colors, blues are cool colors. Collect revive tokens (they show up more often right after someone dies): every 3 brings a fallen teammate back. A revived snake blinks where it will appear for 3 seconds, then is protected for 3 more.

Between rounds, or while you're out, use the ‹ › arrows by your name to pick your color. In team mode, between rounds, the arrows go through the red shades then the blue shades, so picking a color picks your team.

Bonus tiles show up often in battle rounds; the host picks which kind. Fruit (default): collect any fruit; every 3 bananas = +12 length, every 3 cherries = everyone else loses a third, every 3 oranges = 2 random special items. Letters: collect 4 that can be arranged into a real word (CAKE, SNAP, ...); your rack holds 4 and a 5th pushes out the oldest. The bonus is +12 length, or everyone else loses a third.

Type /spectate in chat to sit out the next rounds and just watch (👁 by your name); type it again to play.
