SNAKEWAR
How to play

Arrows / WASD steer · Space start (host) · Enter chat

Playing alone is classic snake: small board, solid walls, +1 per food, and your score (food eaten) is your high score. With 2+ players: each food grows you by 2, food gets more plentiful as the round goes on, and the field is smaller with 2–3 players. The edges wrap around to the other side. Hitting any snake (yourself included) kills you, and your body breaks into food. Last snake alive wins the round.

Chat is hidden while you're alive in a round; press Enter to open it.
